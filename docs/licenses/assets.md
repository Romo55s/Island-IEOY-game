`public/audio/manifest.json` records stable cue IDs, category, loop behavior, gain, local path, and size cap. Replacing a cue with a recording or downloaded sample requires updating its author, source URL, redistribution license, and attribution before enabling it.

The Meta Piano subset used by decision and ending music is stored in `public/audio/instruments/meta-piano/`. It contains 15 OGG samples from the medium-forte layer; Tone.js repitches nearby notes from those samples. The bundled upstream `README.md`, `manifest.json`, and `LICENSE` record the source, sample specification, and CC0 terms.
